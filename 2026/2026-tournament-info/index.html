2026 Tournament Info

Hi GGSO Community,

As we get closer to the tournament, we would like to give some reminders and updates.

Forms

There are various forms due by Wednesday, December 31st, 2025, so please remind your competitors to complete them as soon as possible. Details can be found here. Completed documents for all team members should be scanned into one PDF and submitted by the coach through this Google Form.

Accessing Info

A compilation of all tournament emails can be found here. A folder with all publicly available documents and information can be found here.

Event Conflicts

The event list can be found here, and the event schedule can be found here. We will follow State-level rules and National-level conflicts for all events.

Self-Scheduling

This year’s self-scheduled events are Boomilever, Bungee Drop, Electric Vehicle, Helicopter, Hovercraft, and Robot Tour. Self-scheduled event sign-ups open on Friday, January 9th at 4:00 PM PT and close on Friday, January 16th at 11:00 PM PT.

Sign-ups will take place via ESUS, and coaches have been emailed their login information. Please let us know if you have any questions or concerns regarding this process (e.g., “coach did not receive ESUS login info”).

Merchandise

GGSO has a new merchandise store, which is now open and accessible here! Show your support for this year’s tournament by purchasing a shirt, hoodie, or sweatshirt!

As always, please feel free to reach out with questions or concerns.
