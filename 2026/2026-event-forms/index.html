Required Event Forms & Merchandise

Hello all,

As we approach the tournament, we’d like to share a few event-specific updates and reminders!

Chemistry Lab, Forensics, & Materials Science

Two Google forms must be completed for the Chemistry Lab, Forensics, and Materials Science events to comply with Stanford policy for lab usage. Both forms must be completed by Friday, January 16, at 11:59 PM PT.

  • All coaches should complete the GGSO Lab Safety form, submitting one response per team. Coaches should forward the information in this form to students, as they will need it to complete the second form (the UTL Lab Safety form).

  • Next, each Chemistry Lab, Forensics, and Materials Science competitor must submit the UTL Lab Safety form. Detailed submission instructions can be found within the GGSO Lab Safety form.

Engineering CAD

Before the competition, all Engineering CAD competitors must create an Onshape account. Once registered, please have students submit their account information in this Google form (one response per team). As a reminder, all competitors are expected to provide their own laptop and mouse for the event.

Entomology

Event modification: Each team should bring 2 Class II calculators to this event.

Accessibility

Due to space constraints on campus, some event rooms may not be ADA-accessible. If there are students on your team(s) who require accessibility accommodations, please let us know which events they are participating in ASAP so we can ensure that these events are assigned to ADA-compliant spaces.

Merchandise

Lastly, show support for this year’s tournament by purchasing GGSO 2026 merchandise! If you are interested in buying a shirt, crewneck, or hoodie, our apparel store is located here. We are also excited to introduce stickers and totes, available at this link.

We’re looking forward to seeing you all later this month! Please don’t hesitate to contact us with any questions.
