# Final Tournament Reminders

Hi GGSO coaches,

We hope you’re as excited for the tournament as we are! With the tournament less than 24 hours away, we are sending some final reminders and information before check-in begins. 

Most importantly, please note that all information can be found in the **2026 Coaches Packet, linked <a target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eJzRAeA-_mUvcqyvbOitKmGj471CuKcvoFHvsQkk42Q/edit?usp=sharing">here</a>. Several updates have been made to this packet since it was first sent, so please review it again.** Quick links to specific information in the packet are located on our <a target="_blank" href="https://goldengateso.com/details/">website</a>, and we encourage you to share this with your students, as the information in the packet and emails can be quite dense.

* Please be sure to check the **<a target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eJzRAeA-_mUvcqyvbOitKmGj471CuKcvoFHvsQkk42Q/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.xc8d02e6iqz4">Homeroom Assignments</a>** posted in the Coaches Packet. **Assignments have changed since our 1/26 update.** Teams that were not assigned a homeroom may set up tents in the indicated areas on the Oval Ears or Lomita Mall lawn – please let us know if you have any concerns. 

* Please note that all homerooms and events listed as Roble are located in **Roble Gym** – not Roble Hall.

* **Check-in for coaches** will be in **Sapp (STLC) Commons starting at 8:00 AM**. Impound begins at 8:15 AM for all events. 

* If your team has a homeroom, we ask that you **finish cleaning and vacate your homeroom by 4:45 PM**. The homeroom buildings will be locked after this time, so please be sure to not leave any belongings in your homeroom.

* Coaches can pick up **graded exams and score sheets** from **Shriram UTL 112** after the Awards Ceremony, **provided that homerooms and base camps are in satisfactory condition. Arbitration** has been moved to this room as well.

* **Remind** will be our primary mode of communication during tournament day. To join our class, text the code **@ggso2026** to the number 81010 or input the code into the <a target="_blank" href="https://www.remind.com/join">join site</a>. A maximum of two coaches per team may join – **please make sure to do this by tomorrow morning!** 

* We’re excited to announce that several organizations **will have booths** at GGSO! Check out **STLC Commons** throughout the day to meet representatives from Stanford seeME, Stanford Materials Research Society, Stanford Optical Society, Stanford American Institute of Chemical Engineers, and the Society of Physics Students at Berkeley!

* Our team has prepared a **GGSO Scavenger Hunt** for students to participate in during the day. Tackle a fun puzzle challenge! Swing by the marketing table in STLC Commons for all the details.

* In addition to reviewing the Coaches Packet, please read the <a target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1cleD3_EcOz15C61k1EvFHJCLvN7xlptUl2mB-ZMMrHA/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.q8nicqvdebnz">1/26 Tournament Details</a> update in preparation for the tournament. The announcement has been updated to reflect room changes, and it is also appended to this email for your convenience. Let us know if you have any questions or concerns!

We’ll see you all tomorrow!
