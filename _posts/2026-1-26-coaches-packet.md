# Coaches Packet & Tournament Details

Hello coaches,

The tournament is coming up at the end of this week! In preparation for the 2026 Golden Gate Science Olympiad Invitational on **Saturday, January 31st**, we would like to share some important tournament details. As a reminder, you can view prior major announcements <a target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1cleD3_EcOz15C61k1EvFHJCLvN7xlptUl2mB-ZMMrHA/edit?usp=drive_link">here</a>.

**You can view the Coaches Packet <a target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eJzRAeA-_mUvcqyvbOitKmGj471CuKcvoFHvsQkk42Q/edit?usp=sharing">here</a>. Please review the packet carefully and in its entirety.** 

A summary of its contents is below:

### Remind

Remind will be our **primary mode of communication** during tournament day (though we will also follow important Remind announcements with an email). To join our class, text the code **@ggso2026** to the number 81010 or input the code into the <a target="_blank" href="https://www.remind.com/join">join site</a>. **A maximum of two representatives (coaches, captains) per team may join.**
	
### Schedule

**Check-in begins at 8:00 AM** at Sapp Teaching and Learning Center (STLC Commons), where you will be able to pick up your Coaches Packet that **contains student wristbands** (required for competitors), **arbitration forms**, and all information from the Coaches Packet document linked above. After checking in, head to your <a target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eJzRAeA-_mUvcqyvbOitKmGj471CuKcvoFHvsQkk42Q/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.xc8d02e6iqz4">homeroom</a> or set up your base camp and settle in.

*Note: students must have wristbands on in order to compete, and we will hand out wristbands according to the number of signed liability waivers we receive/have received.*

**See the full <a target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/13BwS3114d_2W2rdAf4Wj8cXTEFh8XwPRMnPAnWADFUo/edit?usp=sharing">event schedule</a>** for our impound and block schedule. It will soon contain a new sheet that outlines what times each team is assigned to for self-scheduled events. 

The **<a target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eJzRAeA-_mUvcqyvbOitKmGj471CuKcvoFHvsQkk42Q/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.y8x5m57qrevj">Awards Ceremony</a>** will take place in Memorial Church. **Doors will open at 4:15 PM**, and due to space constraints, **chairs are reserved for competitors and one coach per team only**. The ceremony will conclude by 7:30 PM, after which coaches can pick up graded exams and score sheets from **STLC 111**. 

*Note: Tests will be released to each team following the Awards Ceremony only if homerooms are in satisfactory condition.*

### Logistics

**The weather looks clear on Saturday**, but please continue monitoring and prepare accordingly for transporting devices, etc.

The **event map** can be viewed via the <a target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eJzRAeA-_mUvcqyvbOitKmGj471CuKcvoFHvsQkk42Q/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.rwkx0bhujpzf">Coaches Packet</a> or <a target="_blank" href="https://tinyurl.com/ggsomap2026">Google Maps</a>. A **parking map** can be found <a target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eJzRAeA-_mUvcqyvbOitKmGj471CuKcvoFHvsQkk42Q/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.wdcs66s1gzbx">here</a>. For check-in, buses may park at Stock Farm Lawn or drop off students at the locations indicated <a target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eJzRAeA-_mUvcqyvbOitKmGj471CuKcvoFHvsQkk42Q/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.9bn8kyz09gc0">here</a>. **Please note that buses cannot enter the Oval, or they may be ticketed and escorted off campus.**

**No food is allowed in homerooms**, and **homerooms must be vacated by 4:45 PM**. Unfortunately, we must enforce the food rule very strictly to avoid facing fines. Note that custodians will be monitoring the homerooms and checking trash bins for food waste. If the weather is as forecasted, we hope your team can grab a bite to eat outside instead — <a target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eJzRAeA-_mUvcqyvbOitKmGj471CuKcvoFHvsQkk42Q/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.dyrhzsx4h4i9">here</a> are some of our **food/drink recommendations**.

**Free WiFi** is available through Stanford Visitor.

Everyone must abide by the **“never alone” policy**, which states that there should never be a minor alone in a room with one adult at any time — nor should a minor ever be forced into a one-on-one situation with a volunteer, event staffer, or other adult. We take this very seriously; if you see a violation of this policy, notify headquarters in STLC 111 immediately.

**Additionally, here are some details regarding Hovercraft and Helicopter:** 
* Hovercraft floor materials are glass and tile. 
* Helicopter ceiling height is about 25 feet.

Thank you for reading through all these reminders and updates. **Please be sure to review the Coaches Packet in its entirety.** As always, don’t hesitate to reach out with any questions, and we look forward to seeing you at Stanford!
