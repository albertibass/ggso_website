# 2026 Tournament Registration

Hi GGSO Community,

This is a reminder that registration for the 2026 Golden Gate Invitational will open **tomorrow, September 20th, 2025 at 12:00 PM PDT** via <a target="_blank" href="https://forms.gle/HxWeZi7rptPjpR6s8">this Google Form</a>. Please note that we are no longer using Scilympiad this year. Spots are limited, so be sure to sign up fast! Our tournament is capped at 60 teams total.

For registered teams, we do ask that the fee of **$305 per team** is paid by **October 17th, 2025**. Payments can be made via PayPal (preferred!) or check. Payment instructions are detailed in the registration Google Form, and they are available for reference in the <a target="_blank" href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1vZWpY3rjyakSRFR82E3vsJ0zxAOF50Eo/view">form preview</a>. If the fee poses a problem for your team, please still register as planned and email us so we can find a solution. Feel free to reach out with any questions; we’re happy to help!
