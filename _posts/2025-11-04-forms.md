# 2026 Required Forms & Submission Deadline

Hi GGSO Community,

We hope you are all doing well and getting excited for Thanksgiving break! As we get closer to the tournament, we would like to give some friendly reminders and updates. 


### Forms

**Every team member** attending the 2026 tournament (including alternates and spectators) must have **two individual forms** on file. These forms should be completed by the team member’s parent or guardian if the team member is under 18.

* Form 1: <a target="_blank" href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/16CUxCbM6SXx1ueR1cvqSirnQFZcPC2YJ/view?usp=sharing">(Individual) GGSO Waiver</a>

* Form 2: <a target="_blank" href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Cj5t6HqumPaqNQFREBfp2S_sSHvnhCb6/view?usp=sharing">(Individual) Stanford Waiver</a>

**Every participating team** must submit a team roster. 

* Form 3: <a target="_blank" href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1B9PDw8aCg_Lm4ooNoE2voImCRJKoARMs/view?usp=sharing">Team Roster</a>


### Form Submission & Deadline

**After collecting all team members’ forms and filling out the team roster, each coach must submit via <a target="_blank" href="https://forms.gle/iFnVL1cxF7jy2ZhW8">this Google Form</a>. The deadline to submit is Wednesday, December 31st, 2025.**

Every school (regardless of how many teams) should fill out the Google Form **exactly once**. In the Google Form, you will be asked to select one of two options: 

* **You may scan all forms and upload them as normal.** Additional instructions for scanning are provided in the Google Form. 

* **You may scan only the team roster(s) and elect to bring physical copies of the waiver forms to us on tournament day.** Simply check the corresponding option on the Google Form and note that, due to strict university regulations, we will only hand out wristbands according to the number of completed forms we have on file. Please only select this option if you are confident that you won’t forget to bring your forms. 

Please note that we will destroy submitted documents immediately after the tournament, in case you have any concerns about the information we are gathering. Please don’t hesitate to reach out if you have any questions!
