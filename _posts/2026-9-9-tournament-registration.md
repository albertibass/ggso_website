# 2027 Tournament Registration Details

Hi GGSO Community,

The 11th annual Golden Gate Science Olympiad Invitational will take place on **Sunday, January 31st, 2027, at UC Berkeley**. We have an update on registration and registration fee details. 

<u>Registration Timeline and Form</u>
The <a target="_blank" href="https://forms.gle/VimwxuH4ZWYZdhGo9">Tournament Registration Form</a> will open on **Saturday, September 19th, 2026, at 12:00 PM PDT**. (You can preview the registration form info and questions at this link.) 

Registration will close on **Saturday, September 26th, 2026, at 12:00 PM PDT**. Please note that as of last year, **we no longer use Scilympiad** for any aspect of the GGSO tournament.

<u>Registration and Waitlist Policies</u>
We will be holding registration on a first-come, first-served basis, with a total of **60 spots available for teams**. When registration is open, there will be an **initial cap of one team per school and 50 spots available for different schools**. If a school would like to register two teams, then its **second team will automatically be placed on the waitlist, ordered based on the time at which the first team was registered**. Any school that does not register within the first 50 slots will also be added to the waitlist as normal, regardless of whether it has a second team. After registration closes, we will take teams from the waitlist to fill the remaining 10 (and any additional open) spots.

<u>Registration Fee</u>
A registration fee of **$305 per team is due Friday, October 16th, 2026**. After October 16th, we will offer 50% refunds on registration fees if a team drops. We will not provide refunds to teams that drop after November 20th. Note that registration closes Saturday, September 26th; we will notify schools of their team status within a few days of the registration closing date. If the fee or timeline poses a problem for your team(s), please still register as planned and email us so we can find a solution that works for you!

<u>Homeroom Notice</u>
We have a very limited number of homerooms to offer this year. Teams will be able to stay on outdoor greenspaces and bring their own tables, chairs, and tents, if desired. **If your school requires a homeroom to attend GGSO 2027, we ask that you complete this <a target="_blank" href="https://forms.gle/BdcR2WYp5ZhSUk6z6">supplemental form</a>**, which opens and closes at the same times as the registration form **(September 19th at 12:00 PM PDT to September 26th at 12:00 PM PDT)**. The time you complete the supplemental form will not affect your school's priority for a spot in our tournament or receiving a homeroom. However, we cannot guarantee homerooms to schools who submit the supplemental form after our registration deadline. (You can <a target="_blank" href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1gcXOZxAAx0sswuQLmx8zyv2BXLBgtJnf/view?usp=sharing">preview the homeroom form info and questions</a> here.) 

<u>Full Timeline</u>
**Saturday, September 19th, 2026, at 12:00 PM PDT** – Registration opens
**Saturday, September 26th, 2026, at 12:00 PM PDT** – Registration closes; team status sent to schools within several days
**Friday, October 16th, 2026** – Registration fee deadline; 50% refunds for drops
**Friday, November 20th, 2026** – No refunds for drops after this time 

As always, please feel free to email us with any questions or concerns!
