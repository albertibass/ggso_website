# 2026 ES/TC Application Due 10/6

Hi GGSO Community,

This is a reminder that the **ES/TC application** for the 10th annual GGSO Invitational at Stanford (hosted on Saturday, January 31st, 2026) is due on **October 6th, 2025, at 11:59 PM PT**. The link for the application can be found <a target="_blank" href="https://forms.gle/tASrYjrijjPcq8yU8">here</a>. Please note that these roles are only available to students who have graduated from high school. 

**If you have prior Science Olympiad experience**, consider applying to be an Event Supervisor (ES) or Test Committee (TC) member. Event Supervisors are responsible for writing the tests and supervising competitors on tournament day, while Test Committee members evaluate test drafts prior to tournament day. 

As an added plus, all in-person volunteers will also receive a **free GGSO t-shirt**, guaranteed transportation to and from Stanford campus on tournament day (for Berkeley students), all-you-can-eat snacks (while supplies last), and free lunch!

Thank you all for your continued interest in running our tournament. Don’t hesitate to contact us with any questions!
