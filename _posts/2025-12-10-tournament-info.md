# 2026 Tournament Info

Hi GGSO Community,

As we get closer to the tournament, we would like to give some reminders and updates.

### Forms

There are various forms due by **Wednesday, December 31st, 2025**, so please remind your competitors to complete them as soon as possible. Details can be found <a target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1cleD3_EcOz15C61k1EvFHJCLvN7xlptUl2mB-ZMMrHA/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.fdt0s45n378q">here</a>. Completed documents for all team members should be scanned into one PDF and submitted by the coach through <a target="_blank" href="https://forms.gle/iFnVL1cxF7jy2ZhW8">this Google Form</a>.


### Accessing Info

A **compilation of all tournament emails** can be found <a target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1cleD3_EcOz15C61k1EvFHJCLvN7xlptUl2mB-ZMMrHA/edit?usp=sharing">here</a>. A folder with all publicly available documents and information can be found <a target="_blank" href="https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1THCre7vZ6Kocrbo5l-d30TXLMwNIwDe5?usp=sharing">here</a>. 


### Event Conflicts

The **event list** can be found <a target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1XFBM1jmN04j74I9hv8p32eE84Dd4ra02SMnXbROdBXo/edit?usp=sharing">here</a>, and the **event schedule** can be found <a target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/13BwS3114d_2W2rdAf4Wj8cXTEFh8XwPRMnPAnWADFUo/edit?usp=sharing">here</a>. We will follow **State-level rules and National-level conflicts** for all events. 


### Self-Scheduling

This year’s self-scheduled events are Boomilever, Bungee Drop, Electric Vehicle, Helicopter, Hovercraft, and Robot Tour. Self-scheduled event sign-ups open on **Friday, January 9th at 4:00 PM PT** and close on **Friday, January 16th at 11:00 PM PT**. 

**Sign-ups will take place via <a target="_blank" href="https://esus.socalscioly.org/home.php?tid=338">ESUS</a>, and coaches have been emailed their login information.** Please let us know if you have any questions or concerns regarding this process (e.g., “coach did not receive ESUS login info”). 


### Merchandise

GGSO has a new merchandise store, which is now open and accessible <a target="_blank" href="https://www.bonfire.com/ggso-invitational-merch/?utm_source=copy_link&utm_medium=campaign_details_share&utm_campaign=ggso-invitational-merch&utm_content=default">here</a>! Show your support for this year’s tournament by purchasing a shirt, hoodie, or sweatshirt!

As always, please feel free to reach out with questions or concerns.
