# Board Applications are Open!

Hello GGSO Community,

The <a target="_blank" href="https://forms.gle/Gd3Ru49ZGA3gPSZm9">application</a> for our **2026-2027 GGSO Board of Directors** is now live!

We’re looking for UC Berkeley and Stanford students, including but not limited to those of the incoming class of 2030, who are passionate about improving the caliber of STEM education and interested in running the 2027 Golden Gate Science Olympiad Invitational. All applicants must have prior Science Olympiad experience, whether through competing, volunteering, or directing tournaments! More information can be found on the application form linked above, including the <a target="_blank" href="https://bit.ly/ggsopositions2026">positions</a> we are actively recruiting for. Applications are due **Saturday, May 30th at 11:59 PM PDT**.

We’re so excited to work with you this upcoming season! As always, please don’t hesitate to contact us if you have any questions.
